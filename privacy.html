Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

🔒 Short version: We don't collect your data. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Ever.

1. No Data Collection

FreeToolHub does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. All 81+ tools on this website run entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your inputs — text, files, calculations — never leave your device.

2. No Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies, session cookies, or any form of persistent identification. The only browser storage used is localStorage for optional features like the Notepad tool (to save your notes locally on your own device), and your theme preference (light/dark mode). This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

3. No Account Required

FreeToolHub requires no registration, no login, and no email address. You can use every tool anonymously.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect minimal technical data:

  • Google Fonts — Loads fonts from Google's CDN. Google may log IP addresses per their privacy policy.
  • Google AdSense — We display ads to keep this site free. Google may use cookies to show relevant ads. You can opt out via Google Ad Settings.
  • Vercel — Our hosting provider. They may collect standard server logs (IP address, request time) as described in their privacy policy.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as page view counts) to understand which tools are most useful. No personally identifiable information is collected in this process.

6. Children's Privacy

FreeToolHub is a general-purpose tool website suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach us at: hello@freetoolhub.app
