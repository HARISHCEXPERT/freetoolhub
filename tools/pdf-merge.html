About the PDF Merger

The PDF Merger lets you combine multiple PDF files into a single document — instantly, privately, and for free. Drop your files in any order, rearrange them, and download the merged PDF in seconds. No sign-up, no watermarks, no file size limits imposed by a server.

How to use the PDF Merger

  1. Click the drop zone or drag your PDF files in.
  2. Your files appear as a list — remove any you do not want.
  3. Click Merge PDFs to combine them in order.
  4. The merged PDF downloads automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF Merger free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up, no limits and no watermarks.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser using pdf-lib. Your files never leave your device.

How many PDFs can I merge?

There is no hard limit — merge as many as your browser memory allows. For very large files, merge in batches.

Will the merged PDF preserve bookmarks and formatting?

Yes — the tool copies pages directly, so all text, images, and formatting are preserved.
