About the Case Converter

The Case Converter is a free online tool that lets you uPPER, lower, Title, camelCase, snake_case & more — making it ideal for writers, students and editors handling large amounts of text. Everything runs locally in your browser, so it is fast, completely private, and works even when you are offline.

Whether you are a writer, blogger or student, the Case Converter gives you instant results with no installation, no account and no limits.

How to use the Case Converter

  1. Paste or type your content into the input box on the left.
  2. Click the action button for what you need.
  3. Copy the result from the output box, or download it as a file.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Case Converter free to use?

Yes. The Case Converter is completely free with no sign-up, no usage limits and no watermarks. Use it as many times as you like.

Is my data safe and private?

Absolutely. The Case Converter processes everything inside your browser — your input is never uploaded to any server, so your data stays 100% private.

Is there a limit on how much text I can paste?

There is no hard limit. Because everything runs locally in your browser, you can process very large blocks of text quickly without any upload or sign-up.

Do I need to install anything or use it on mobile?

No installation needed. The Case Converter works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet and mobile.

What does the Case Converter do?

It helps you uPPER, lower, Title, camelCase, snake_case & more quickly and accurately, with a clean interface and one-click copy or download of the result.
