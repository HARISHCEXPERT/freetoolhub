About the ROI Calculator

The ROI Calculator is a free online tool that lets you return on investment — helping you plan loans, savings and investments with instant, accurate numbers. Everything runs locally in your browser, so it is fast, completely private, and works even when you are offline.

Whether you are planning a loan, SIP or budget, the ROI Calculator gives you instant results with no installation, no account and no limits.

How to use the ROI Calculator

  1. Enter your values into the input fields.
  2. Click Calculate (results also update as you type).
  3. Read the breakdown shown below the form.
