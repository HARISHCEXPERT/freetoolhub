About the Remove Emojis

The Remove Emojis is a free online tool that lets you strip emoji & pictographs from text — making it ideal for writers, students and editors handling large amounts of text. Everything runs locally in your browser, so it is fast, completely private, and works even when you are offline.

Whether you are a writer, blogger or student, the Remove Emojis gives you instant results with no installation, no account and no limits.

How to use the Remove Emojis

  1. Paste or type your content into the input box on the left.
  2. Click the action button for what you need.
  3. Copy the result from the output box, or download it as a file.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Remove Emojis free to use?

Yes. The Remove Emojis is completely free with no sign-up, no usage limits and no watermarks. Use it as many times as you like.

Is my data safe and private?

Absolutely. The Remove Emojis processes everything inside your browser — your input is never uploaded to any server, so your data stays 100% private.

Is there a limit on how much text I can paste?

There is no hard limit. Because everything runs locally in your browser, you can process very large blocks of text quickly without any upload or sign-up.

Do I need to install anything or use it on mobile?

No installation needed. The Remove Emojis works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet and mobile.

What does the Remove Emojis do?

It helps you strip emoji & pictographs from text quickly and accurately, with a clean interface and one-click copy or download of the result.
