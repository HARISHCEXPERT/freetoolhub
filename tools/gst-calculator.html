Frequently asked questions

What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a unified indirect tax in India that replaced multiple taxes. It has four main slabs: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%.

How to calculate GST?

To add GST: Amount × (1 + GST rate/100). To remove GST from inclusive price: Amount / (1 + GST rate/100). Our calculator does this automatically.

Is this GST calculator free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no login, no limits. Works in your browser with no data sent to any server.

What GST rate applies to services?

Most services in India attract 18% GST. Some essential services are exempt or taxed at 5%. Professional services like IT, consulting typically fall under 18%.

What is CGST and SGST?

CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) are both halves of the GST rate for intra-state transactions. For an 18% GST transaction, CGST = 9% and SGST = 9%.
