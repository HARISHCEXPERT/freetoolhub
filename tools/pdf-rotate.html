About the PDF Page Rotator

The PDF Page Rotator fixes incorrectly oriented pages in seconds. Choose 90°, 180° or 270° rotation and apply it to all pages or just the ones you specify. Quality is never affected since only the rotation metadata changes.

How to use the PDF Page Rotator

  1. Drop or select your PDF file.
  2. Choose the rotation angle.
  3. Optionally enter specific page numbers to rotate.
  4. Click Rotate & Download.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only specific pages?

Yes — enter page numbers like 1,3,5-8 to rotate just those pages. Leave blank to rotate all pages.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no account needed.

Will the PDF quality be affected?

No. The tool only changes page rotation metadata — no re-encoding happens, so quality is perfectly preserved.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser.
