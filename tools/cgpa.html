About the CGPA Calculator

The CGPA Calculator is a free online tool that lets you weighted CGPA from semesters — so you can track grades, attendance and scores in seconds. Everything runs locally in your browser, so it is fast, completely private, and works even when you are offline.

Whether you are a school or college student, the CGPA Calculator gives you instant results with no installation, no account and no limits.

How to use the CGPA Calculator

  1. Enter your values into the input fields.
  2. Click Calculate (results also update as you type).
  3. Read the breakdown shown below the form.

Frequently asked questions

Is the CGPA Calculator free to use?

Yes. The CGPA Calculator is completely free with no sign-up, no usage limits and no watermarks. Use it as many times as you like.

Is my data safe and private?

Absolutely. The CGPA Calculator processes everything inside your browser — your input is never uploaded to any server, so your data stays 100% private.

Can I use this for my school or college grading?

Yes. The tool follows common grading and percentage conventions and lets you enter your own credits, marks or required percentage, so it adapts to most boards and universities.

Do I need to install anything or use it on mobile?

No installation needed. The CGPA Calculator works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet and mobile.

What does the CGPA Calculator do?

It helps you weighted CGPA from semesters quickly and accurately, with a clean interface and one-click copy or download of the result.
