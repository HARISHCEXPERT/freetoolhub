Notes: New regime is the default regime from FY 2023-24 onwards. New regime rebate under Sec 87A makes income up to ₹12,00,000 tax-free (₹12,75,000 for salaried, including standard deduction) from FY 2025-26. Old regime rebate applies up to ₹5,00,000 taxable income. Standard deduction ₹75,000 (new regime) / ₹50,000 (old regime) auto-applied for salaried/pension income. Presumptive income under 44AD/44ADA is not eligible for most Chapter VI-A style business deductions beyond what's built in — this tool applies standard personal deductions against total income per old regime rules.
