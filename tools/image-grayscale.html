About the Grayscale & Filters

The Grayscale & Filters is a free online tool that lets you grayscale, sepia, invert, blur — letting you edit and convert pictures privately, right on your device. Everything runs locally in your browser, so it is fast, completely private, and works even when you are offline.

Whether you are working with photos or screenshots, the Grayscale & Filters gives you instant results with no installation, no account and no limits.

How to use the Grayscale & Filters

  1. Click the drop zone or drag an image file (PNG, JPG, WEBP) onto the page.
  2. Adjust the available options to get the result you want.
  3. Press the action button to process the image in your browser.
  4. Click Download to save the new image to your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Grayscale & Filters free to use?

Yes. The Grayscale & Filters is completely free with no sign-up, no usage limits and no watermarks. Use it as many times as you like.

Absolutely. The Grayscale & Filters processes everything inside your browser — your input is never uploaded to any server, so your data stays 100% private.

No installation needed. The Grayscale & Filters works in any modern browser on desktop, tablet and mobile.
