Frequently asked questions

Is the image compressor free?

Yes, 100% free. Compress unlimited images with no watermarks or signup required.

No. All compression happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats are supported for compression.

Typically 40-80% file size reduction is achievable while maintaining good visual quality. Results vary by image content.
