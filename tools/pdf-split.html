About the PDF Splitter

The PDF Splitter lets you extract individual pages or split a large PDF into smaller chunks — all inside your browser with no upload. Perfect for sharing only specific pages of a document or breaking a large report into sections.

How to use the PDF Splitter

  1. Drop or select your PDF file.
  2. Enter page numbers to extract (e.g. 1,3,5-8) or set a split size (e.g. every 2 pages).
  3. Click Extract / Split.
  4. Your files download automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF Splitter free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up or watermarks.

Can I extract specific pages?

Yes — enter a range like 1,3,5-8 to extract exactly the pages you need.

Can I split into equal chunks?

Yes — use the "split every N pages" option to divide your PDF into equal parts automatically.

Are my files uploaded?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.
