Frequently asked questions

Is the resume builder free?

Yes, completely free. Build and download your resume as PDF with no watermarks and no signup required.

Can I download my resume as PDF?

Yes. Use your browser's Print to PDF feature to save your resume as a PDF file. Click the Print/Download button and select "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Is my data saved?

Your resume data is stored temporarily in your browser session. Download your PDF before closing the tab. No data is sent to any server.

What resume format does this use?

The resume builder creates a clean, ATS-friendly single-page format suitable for most job applications in India and internationally.

Can I create multiple resumes?

Yes. You can create different versions of your resume by modifying the content and downloading each version as a separate PDF.
