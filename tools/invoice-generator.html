Is the invoice generator free?

Yes, completely free for freelancers and small businesses. No signup, no watermarks, unlimited invoices.

How do I download the invoice as PDF?

Click the Print/Download PDF button. In the print dialog, select "Save as PDF" as the destination. Set margins to minimum for best results.

Does it support GST?

Yes. The invoice generator supports all GST rates (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and shows CGST + SGST breakdown for intra-state transactions.
