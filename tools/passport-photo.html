Frequently asked questions

Is the passport photo maker free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no watermarks, no limits.

What size is a standard Indian passport photo?

Indian passport photos are 35mm × 45mm with a white background. The face should occupy 70-80% of the frame.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. All photo processing happens in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photo never leaves your device.

Can I use this for visa applications?

Yes. The tool creates standard passport/visa size photos. Check the specific size requirements for your target country's visa.

What background color is required?

Most passport and visa applications require a plain white or off-white background. The tool helps you set the correct background.
