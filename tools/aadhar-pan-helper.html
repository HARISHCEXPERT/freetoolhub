Aadhar & PAN Form Helper

Step-by-step guide to fill Aadhar correction, new PAN card, and PAN address change forms correctly

You can correct Name, DOB, Address, Mobile, Gender in Aadhar — online or offline both options available.
Option 1: Online (Fastest)
1
Go to UIDAI Self Service Portal
Visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in → Login with your Aadhar number + OTP on registered mobile
2
Click "Update Aadhaar Online"
Select what you want to update — Name / DOB / Gender / Address / Language
3
Upload Supporting Document
Scan or photo of valid document. Must be clear and all 4 corners visible.
4
Pay ₹50 fee
Pay via UPI, NetBanking, or Debit Card. Get URN (Update Request Number) for tracking.
Open UIDAI Portal ↗
Option 2: Aadhar Seva Kendra (Offline)
1
Find nearest Aadhar Kendra
Go to uidai.gov.in → "Locate Enrolment & Update Centre" → Enter your PIN code
2
Fill Aadhar Correction Form
Fill form in BLOCK LETTERS only. Use blue or black pen. Do not use pencil.
3
Attach Documents + Visit Centre
Carry original + 1 photocopy of proof document. Biometric will be taken again.
Accepted Documents for Address Proof:
Passport Bank Passbook Voter ID Electricity Bill (3 months) Gas Bill Water Bill Ration Card Rent Agreement
Common Mistakes to Avoid:
• Name must match exactly with supporting document
• Date of Birth: use DD/MM/YYYY format
• Mobile number change is NOT possible online — must visit Kendra
• Address proof must show your current address
New PAN card application is done through NSDL or UTIITSL portals. Fee is ₹107 (India) or ₹1,017 (abroad).
Online PAN Application — Form 49A
1
Go to NSDL PAN Portal
Visit onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html → Select "New PAN - Indian Citizen (Form 49A)"
2
Fill Personal Details
Name as per Aadhar, Father's name, DOB, Gender, Source of Income, Address
3
Upload Photo & Signature
Photo: JPG, max 50KB, white background. Signature: JPG, max 30KB, white background.
4
e-KYC with Aadhar
Link Aadhar for paperless verification — OTP will come on Aadhar-registered mobile
5
Pay ₹107 & Submit
Get acknowledgement number. PAN delivered in 15-20 working days. e-PAN in 2-3 days.
Apply on NSDL ↗

PAN correction (name, DOB, address) is done via Form CSF — same NSDL or UTIITSL portal. Fee ₹107.
PAN Correction — Form CSF
1
Visit NSDL Portal
Go to onlineservices.nsdl.com → Select "Changes or Correction in existing PAN"
2
Enter Existing PAN Number
Fill your current PAN. Only tick the fields you want to change — do NOT touch others.
3
Enter Corrected Information
Fill the corrected name/DOB/address exactly as in your supporting document.
4
Upload Proof Documents
Upload document supporting the correction. e.g. Aadhar for name/DOB correction.
5
Pay & Submit
Pay ₹107. Corrected PAN card delivered in 15-20 days.
Open NSDL Portal ↗
Important: If correcting name, it must match your Aadhar exactly. If both Aadhar and PAN have wrong name, correct Aadhar first.
New Aadhar enrollment is free. You must visit an Aadhar Enrolment Centre — it cannot be done online.
New Aadhar Enrollment Steps
1
Find Aadhar Enrolment Centre
Go to uidai.gov.in → "Locate Enrolment Centre" → Enter PIN code. Common centres: Post offices, Banks, CSC centres.
2
Carry Documents
Identity + Address proof (same document can work for both — Passport, Voter ID, etc.)
3
Fill Enrollment Form at Centre
Form available at the centre. Fill in BLOCK LETTERS. Provide active mobile number.
4
Biometrics Captured
10 fingerprints + iris scan + photo taken at the centre. Takes 5-10 minutes.
5
Get Enrolment Slip
You'll get a slip with Enrolment ID. Aadhar delivered in 90 days. Track on UIDAI website.

Common Questions
How many days does Aadhar correction take? +
Online correction takes 5-7 working days. Offline at Aadhar Kendra takes 10-15 working days. You'll get SMS updates on your registered mobile.
Can I have 2 PAN cards? +
No — having 2 PAN cards is illegal and carries a penalty of ₹10,000. If you accidentally got 2, surrender the duplicate one at your nearest Income Tax office.
Is e-PAN valid? +
Yes, e-PAN (digital PAN) is 100% valid and legally equal to physical PAN card. You can download it from the NSDL or UTIITSL website anytime for free.
What if mobile number is not linked to Aadhar? +
You cannot do online Aadhar updates without a registered mobile. You must visit an Aadhar Seva Kendra to first register your mobile number.
