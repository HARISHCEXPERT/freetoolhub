About the PDF Page Numbers

The PDF Page Numbers tool adds clean, professional page numbers to any PDF. Choose from multiple positions (bottom center, bottom right, etc.) and formats (1, Page 1, or 1 of 10). Set a custom starting number for documents that begin partway through a series.

How to use the PDF Page Numbers

  1. Drop or select your PDF file.
  2. Choose position, format and starting number.
  3. Click Add Page Numbers.
  4. The numbered PDF downloads automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose where the page numbers appear?

Yes — bottom center, bottom right, bottom left, or top center.

Can I start from a number other than 1?

Yes — set any starting number.

What formats are available?

Plain numbers (1, 2, 3), "Page 1, Page 2", or "1 of 10" format.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
