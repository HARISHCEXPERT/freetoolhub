Photo or signature rejected on a government form? Check these 7 things

Updated 2026-10-20 · FreeToolHub

You compressed the picture, the upload box still says "invalid". Most of the time it is one of the checks below, and the file size is often not the culprit. Go through them in order.

1. The file is too small, not too big

Many portals set a range, for example 20 KB to 100 KB, not only a maximum. A plain signature on white paper compresses very well and can end up at 8 KB, which gets rejected. If the form shows a minimum, check your result against it, not just against the maximum.

On our Compress Image to Exact KB tool, the number you type is a maximum. The tool picks the highest quality that fits under it, so the result can land lower. If it lands below the form's minimum, type a bigger width and height (more pixels means a bigger file) and compress again. We do not enforce a minimum for you yet, so read the size shown under the result.

2. KB, not MB, and 1 KB is 1024 bytes

Forms say KB. Your phone gallery often shows MB. A 2 MB photo is about 2048 KB. Right-click the file and check "Size", not "Size on disk".

3. Wrong format or just a renamed extension

If the form wants JPG, a PNG renamed to .jpg can fail on some portals. Convert it properly. iPhone HEIC photos do not open in every browser, so convert them to JPG first. Our compressor always saves a real JPG.

4. Pixel dimensions do not match

Some forms specify pixels or centimetres as well. Our tool lets you type a width and a height. When you fill both, the picture is cropped to fill that size and is not stretched, so keep your face or signature near the middle before you upload.

5. The photo is sideways or the face is too small

Portals that match your photo to your face care about the frame. Take the picture again with the camera at eye level, plain light background, and your face filling most of the frame, rather than cropping a distant photo.

6. Filename and spaces

Some portals only accept simple names, such as photo.jpg and signature.jpg, without spaces or brackets. Rename the file after downloading.

7. Signature on the wrong background or in capitals

Use dark ink on plain white paper, scan or photograph in good light, and crop out the shadows. Many boards also ask that you sign the way you normally do, so a stylised or all-capital signature can be refused when it does not match your other records.

Always read your own notification. Sizes and rules change between exams and from year to year. The numbers in your official notice win over any website, including this one.
