Indian passport photo size: 4.5 x 3.5 cm, and when you need a photo at all

Last checked 2026-10-20 · FreeToolHub

Most "passport photo size" pages jump straight to pixels and KB. The official application form instruction booklet on passportindia.gov.in says something more useful first: whether you need a photo at all.

Do you need a photo?

Per that booklet, a photograph is not required for applications submitted at a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office PSK. It is required for applications through a District Passport Cell, Speed Post Centre or Citizen Service Centre. The booklet carries no date or version note, so confirm on passportindia.gov.in before you spend money on prints.

What the booklet asks for in the photo

ItemAs stated in the booklet
Size4.5 cm long by 3.5 cm wide, one recent colour photo that fits the box
PrintGood photo paper. Computer prints and black-and-white photos are not accepted
Background and clothingPlain white background, dark clothing. No dark backgrounds or uniforms
FaceFull face, frontal, head centred, both ears visible, both eyes open, natural expression
GlassesNo glare on lenses, and dark or coloured glasses that hide the eyes are not accepted
OtherDo not sign on the photo. No shadows on the face or background. Photos cut from group pictures are not accepted

About the online upload. The booklet does not give any KB limit or pixel size for uploading a photo online, so we are not going to guess one. If the portal shows a size at the upload step, use that.

Where our tools fit in

4.5 cm by 3.5 cm is the same as 45 mm by 35 mm. Our Passport Size Photo Maker makes photos in the 35 by 45 mm format with a white background, which is also what many other ID forms ask for.
