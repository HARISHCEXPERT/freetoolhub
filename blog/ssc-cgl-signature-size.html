SSC CGL signature size, and why there is no photo to upload

Last checked 2026-10-20 · FreeToolHub

Older guides tell you to compress your passport photo for SSC. According to a 2026 summary, the CGL photo is now captured live by webcam or phone camera while you fill the form, so there is no photo file to upload. The signature is still an upload. We could not verify these numbers in the official SSC notice ourselves, so confirm them in your exam's notice on ssc.gov.in.

Reported
PhotographLive capture, no upload. Good light, plain background, face straight to the camera, no cap or mask.
Signature formatJPG
Signature size10 KB to 20 KB
Signature dimensionsAbout 4.0 cm wide and 2.0 cm high

Source: Physics Wallah, 25 May 2026. That page does not name the exact SSC notice it comes from, which is why we ask you to check the notice itself.

Do not go too small. The floor is 10 KB. A simple signature on white paper can shrink below that. After compressing, read the size under the result, and if it is under 10 KB, enlarge the width and height a little and run it again.

Compress your signature in a few steps

  1. Sign with dark ink on plain white paper and photograph or scan it in good light. Crop out shadows and empty margins.
  2. Open Compress Signature to 20 KB. The target is already set to 20.
  3. Optionally type a width and height in the same 2:1 shape as the 4 cm by 2 cm area.
  4. Compress, check that the size is between 10 and 20 KB, and download.

Other sizes if your notice says something different: 10 KB, 15 KB, 30 KB.
