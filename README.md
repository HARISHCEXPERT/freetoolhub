# FreeToolHub

**Live site:** [freetoolhub.app](https://freetoolhub.app)

114+ free, browser-based tools — no login, no signup, no data ever leaves your device. Everything runs client-side in vanilla JavaScript.

## What's inside

- **Developer Tools** — JSON Formatter, Regex Tester, JWT Decoder, Base64, Hash Generator, UUID Generator, and more
- **Science & Math Tools** — Chemical Equation Balancer, Molecular Weight Calculator, Periodic Table, Physics Formula Reference, Trigonometry Calculator, Statistics Calculator
- **Finance Tools** — EMI, SIP, FD, RD, GST, Compound Interest calculators
- **PDF Tools** — Merge, Split, Rotate, Watermark, PDF to Word
- **Text, Image, SEO & India-specific tools**

## Why

Most free tool sites are bloated with ads, require sign-up, or quietly upload your data to a server. FreeToolHub runs entirely in your browser — nothing is ever sent anywhere.

## Tech stack

Vanilla JavaScript, static HTML, no backend, no build step for most tools.
